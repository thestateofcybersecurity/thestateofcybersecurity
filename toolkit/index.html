Encrypted email from Switzerland with PGP built in, no key wrangling required. End-to-end encryption only applies Proton-to-Proton or with PGP contacts; mail to ordinary inboxes travels like ordinary email.

NordVPN

sponsored

Audited no-logs VPN for public Wi-Fi and untrusted networks. It encrypts traffic in transit; it does not stop phishing or malware.
